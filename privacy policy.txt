Depending on how you use the App, information may be stored locally on your device, including:

Deleting the App may remove locally stored App data, subject to Apple's operating-system behavior and any data you may have separately backed up.

## 4. Analytics

### Supabase analytics

We use Supabase, a hosted database service, to store product analytics so we can understand how users interact with Historical Wisdom and improve the App.

The information sent to our analytics database may include:

* a random, app-specific user identifier (`user_id`);
* App launches;
* onboarding events and which onboarding or paywall screens were shown (screen identifiers such as `welcome`, `themes`, or `paywall_plans` — these identify screens, not your display name);
* onboarding experiment assignment (for example an experiment id and variant such as `control` or `challenger_v1`);
* the acquisition source selected by the user;
* paywall views and related purchase-flow events (including spin-wheel or offer-related events where shown);
* purchase attempts and purchase results;
* restore taps;
* product identifiers for subscriptions or one-time unlocks;
* whether onboarding has been completed or the user has reached the main app after onboarding;
* whether Premium is currently active;
* review-prompt related events (for example whether a rating prompt was eligible, shown, or declined);
* App labels we attach to events, including `app` (“Historical Wisdom”) and `platform` (“iOS”).

The App does not send your display name, email address, phone number, saved quotes, search history, selected quote collections, notification content, or location to our analytics database.

We do not intentionally use analytics to determine your city or precise location. We only send the custom events described in this policy.

### Aggregate counters

We may also store anonymous product counters (for example counts of event names by app version) that are **not** tied to your `user_id`. Those aggregate counters may remain after you use **Delete My Data**.

### Analytics purposes

We use analytics to:

* understand how users discover and use the App;
* measure onboarding completion and activation;
* understand interaction with Premium/paywalls;
* measure subscription conversion and restore activity;
* identify technical or product areas that should be improved;
* evaluate the effectiveness of acquisition sources and onboarding experiments.

We do not sell analytics data.

## 5. In-app feedback

Historical Wisdom may let you send product feedback from inside the App (for example Profile → Feedback, or after declining a rating prompt) and may let you vote on individual quotes.

Feedback submissions may include:

* a random, app-specific user identifier;
* feedback source (for example profile or review decline);
* selected topic or intent chips;
* optional free-text notes you choose to write;
* for quote votes: the vote and quote identifier (and related content identifiers needed to understand the vote);
* app version and build;
* iOS version and device model;
* language setting;
* whether Premium is active.

We use this information only to improve Historical Wisdom and respond to product issues. We do not use feedback submissions for advertising tracking.

## 6. Subscriptions and RevenueCat

Historical Wisdom may offer optional auto-renewable subscriptions and/or a one-time Lifetime unlock through Apple's App Store.

Apple processes the App Store transaction and payment. We do not receive or store your full payment card number or other payment credentials used by Apple to process your purchase.

We use RevenueCat as a subscription-management service to determine subscription status and entitlements.

RevenueCat may process information such as:

* an anonymous or app-specific RevenueCat user identifier;
* subscription and entitlement status;
* App Store purchase and receipt-related information;
* product identifiers;
* trial or introductory-offer eligibility;
* restore information;
* relevant SDK/device information;
* the acquisition source attribute that we assign when you provide that information.

RevenueCat is used to provide subscription functionality and determine whether Premium access should be available.

RevenueCat acts as a service provider/processor for customer data we provide to it, subject to the applicable contractual and privacy arrangements. RevenueCat states that customer deletion through its dashboard or API can be used to satisfy GDPR erasure requests.

## 12. Data deletion

You can request deletion of data associated with your use of Historical Wisdom.

The App provides a **Delete My Data** function under **Profile → Privacy**.

When you use this function, the App captures only the identifiers necessary to identify the relevant records:

* the Wisdom analytics `user_id` associated with the installation; and
* the RevenueCat `app_user_id` associated with the installation.

These identifiers are sent to our secure deletion service solely to process the deletion request.

### What we delete

Our deletion service deletes, for the specified identity:

* analytics profile and event rows in Supabase;
* onboarding screen-reach rows linked to that identity;
* in-app feedback submissions linked to that identity;
* the RevenueCat customer record associated with that identifier (subject to RevenueCat processing).

The App also rotates its local analytics identifier so that the deleted identifier is no longer used for subsequent analytics.

### What deletion does not do

Deleting your Historical Wisdom analytics, feedback, and RevenueCat customer data does **not**:

* cancel an active Apple subscription;
* delete Apple's independent App Store transaction records;
* delete anonymous aggregate product counters that are not tied to your `user_id`.

You must cancel an active subscription through Apple's subscription-management system.

If you continue using Historical Wisdom while an Apple subscription remains active, your Premium entitlement may be restored and a new RevenueCat customer record may be created for the new app identifier.

We may retain limited information necessary to document that a deletion request was received and processed, or where retention is required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, accounting, or dispute-resolution purposes.
