## 4. Analytics

### Mixpanel

We use Mixpanel, a product analytics service, to understand how users interact with Historical Wisdom and to improve the App. Our Mixpanel project is configured for **EU data residency** (events are sent to Mixpanel’s EU API endpoint).

The information sent to Mixpanel may include:

* a random, app-specific user identifier (`user_id`);
* App launches;
* onboarding events and onboarding step information (including a step identifier such as `name`, `inspirations`, or `legal` — these identify which onboarding screen was shown, not your display name);
* the acquisition source selected by the user;
* paywall views;
* purchase attempts and purchase results;
* restore taps;
* product identifiers for subscriptions;
* whether onboarding has been completed;
* whether Premium is currently active;
* App labels we attach to events, including `app` (“Historical Wisdom”) and `platform` (“iOS”);
* App and device information supplied by the Mixpanel SDK, such as operating-system version, App version, device model, screen size, and SDK/library information.

The App does not send your display name, email address, phone number, saved quotes, search history, selected quote collections, notification content, or location to Mixpanel.

We have disabled IP-based geographic enrichment for our Mixpanel analytics configuration. We do not intentionally use Mixpanel analytics to determine your city or precise location.

Mixpanel automatic events are disabled. We only send the custom events described in this policy.

### Analytics purposes

We use analytics to:

* understand how users discover and use the App;
* measure onboarding completion;
* understand interaction with Premium/paywalls;
* measure subscription conversion and restore activity;
* identify technical or product areas that should be improved;
* evaluate the effectiveness of acquisition sources selected by users.

We do not sell analytics data.

## 11. Data deletion

You can request deletion of data associated with your use of Historical Wisdom.

The App provides a **Delete My Data** function under **Profile → Privacy**.

When you use this function, the App captures only the identifiers necessary to identify the relevant records:

* the Mixpanel `distinct_id` associated with the installation; and
* the RevenueCat `app_user_id` associated with the installation.

These identifiers are sent to our secure deletion service solely to process the deletion request.

### Mixpanel deletion

Our deletion service submits a deletion request to Mixpanel for the specified analytics identity.

This request covers the associated Mixpanel profile and analytics events belonging to that identity.

Mixpanel may require additional time to complete deletion of historical event data. Where applicable, deletion may take up to approximately 30 days to complete.

The App also resets its local Mixpanel identity so that the deleted identifier is no longer used for subsequent analytics.

### RevenueCat deletion

Our deletion service requests deletion of the specified RevenueCat customer record.

This removes the RevenueCat customer information associated with that identifier, including relevant attributes and purchase/subscription history stored by RevenueCat.

RevenueCat's current API supports customer deletion and may queue the deletion for processing.

### Local identifier reset

After the deletion requests have been successfully submitted, the App removes the previous analytics identifier from local storage and generates a new app-specific identifier if you continue using the App.

This prevents subsequent analytics events from being associated with the previous analytics identity.

### What deletion does not do

Deleting your Historical Wisdom analytics and RevenueCat customer data does **not** cancel an active Apple subscription.

Apple continues to manage an active subscription independently. Deleting the App or using **Delete My Data** does not by itself stop subscription renewal.

You must cancel an active subscription through Apple's subscription-management system.

If you continue using Historical Wisdom while an Apple subscription remains active, your Premium entitlement may be restored and a new RevenueCat customer record may be created for the new app identifier. RevenueCat confirms that deleting a RevenueCat customer does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Apple's independent transaction and legal records may also be retained by Apple according to Apple's policies and legal obligations.
